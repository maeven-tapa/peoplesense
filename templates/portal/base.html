PEOPLESENSE GUIDE

A smarter view of your building

Quick setup guides you through your administrator password, Wi-Fi AP name, USB camera, and counting line.

My camera lets you select the USB webcam, start or stop its preview, and position the counting line. After switching webcams, start the new preview and save its line before Tripwire counting resumes. See Settings for device details.

Surveillance saves a photo when a person is detected, then waits your chosen interval before the next capture. Open Captures to view photos.

Tripwire records entries and exits when tracked people cross the line. Follow camera movement keeps it aligned during small shifts; counting pauses when alignment is uncertain.

This portal manages one local camera. The AP name is saved as device configuration; broadcasting it requires a supported wireless adapter.
